NEW INSTRUMENT scratchpad/w65a8/zeroinit.py -- a per-SYMBOL, order-independent
real-bug screen that ownmap's E5 leg cannot be: E5 reports "N bytes differ" per
WINDOW, so a window whose labels are merely out of ORDER buries the genuine
defects in alignment noise.  zeroinit compares, for every blob label a recon TU
defines, retail's bytes at its retail VA against OUR bytes at its offset in our
section, and reports only the class that cannot be a layout artifact: retail
NON-ZERO, ours ALL-ZERO, no relocation covering our bytes = the object is
initialised in retail and we emitted a zero-valued tentative definition.
This is the class behind W64-A18's aicop/fastrand finds and W64-A18 sec.10's
flare/sndpdsafeloop hand-offs, made mechanical.  Swept all three blob regions
(773 labels): 7 hits, all decoded from rom/nfs4-f.exe and all fixed here.

TWO ANTI-VACUITY DEFECTS IN THE NEW SCREEN, both caught by its own asserts
before any conclusion was drawn, both recorded in-source:
  * `objdump -s` prints the offset at the section VMA's width -- 4 hex digits
    for an unlinked object, not 8.  An 8-digit regex made EVERY read come back
    empty; the assert-to-UNKNOWN path reported 447 UNKNOWN instead of "0
    defects, clean".
  * `set(ours) > {0}` is a SUPERSET test and is FALSE for {0xff}: it reported
    16 already-correct symbols (aispeeds, fastrand, nfs3, force) as defects.
    Fixed to `any(ours)`; 19 -> 3 hits on that region, and the 16 were
    re-verified against the ROM as already byte-correct.
